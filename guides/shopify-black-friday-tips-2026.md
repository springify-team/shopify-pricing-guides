# Shopify Black Friday Tips for 2026

## Quick answer

Springify analyzed 600+ pricing campaigns run through its app during Black Friday week in 2025. In that sample, 66.7% of stores reduced at least some prices before Friday, 20% and 30% were the most common identifiable percentage reductions, and 66.4% of campaigns included scheduled rollback.

Use these patterns as planning references, not evidence of which strategy generates more revenue. The analysis describes observed pricing activity, not campaign performance or all Shopify merchants.

## Choose the start deliberately

Black Friday was November 28 in 2025. Each store's first observed reduction during the analyzed week was distributed as follows:

| First observed reduction | Share of stores |
| --- | ---: |
| Monday–Wednesday | 42.9% |
| Thursday | 23.8% |
| Black Friday | 22.6% |
| Saturday–Cyber Monday | 10.7% |

The combined pre-Friday share was 66.7%. That does not mean those stores officially launched promotions early: a price change does not establish marketing intent, and some promotions may have started before the observation window.

For 2026 planning, Friday morning is one possible start, not a requirement suggested by this sample.

## Treat common reductions as reference points

Among campaigns where a percentage reduction could be identified:

| Price reduction | Share of those campaigns |
| --- | ---: |
| 20% | 21.2% |
| 30% | 16.2% |
| 50% | 10.2% |

The median reduction was 25%. These frequencies do not show that a particular percentage performed better. Choose a reduction that fits your margins, products, and promotion.

## Define what belongs in the sale

Campaign targeting was more often selective than storewide:

| Target | Share of campaigns |
| --- | ---: |
| Collections | 41.4% |
| Specific products | 34.8% |
| Entire store | 11.8% |
| Selected variants or filtering rules | Remaining campaigns |

Also, 65.7% of campaigns affected 100 variants or fewer. One store can run multiple campaigns for separate collections or reduction levels, so a small campaign does not necessarily mean a small store or promotion.

Decide whether seasonal collections, clearance, selected high-margin products, or already-discounted items need different pricing. Preview the final variant selection and exclusions. The figures describe segmentation choices; they do not prove that targeted campaigns outperform storewide sales.

## Leave time to review

Among scheduled campaigns with usable timing data, 35.4% were created at least one day before the planned start. Most were configured less than a day ahead.

This does not establish that earlier preparation improves results. Its practical value is leaving time to verify included variants, Price, Compare-at price, exclusions, start and end times, and potential conflicts with apps, imports, or integrations that edit the same fields.

## Plan the end and restoration

Scheduled rollback appeared in 66.4% of campaigns. Rollback activity clustered around the end of Cyber Monday and the following day.

There was no single standard campaign length. Among campaigns already started before Cyber Monday, 26.6% were scheduled to remain active through December 2, 2025 or later. This is scheduled duration, not confirmation that all those campaigns actually stayed active.

Set the end deliberately and restore saved values instead of applying the same percentage in reverse:

```text
$100 reduced by 20% = $80
$80 increased by 20% = $96
Restoring the saved original Price returns it to $100.
```

See [How to Schedule Shopify Sale Prices](schedule-shopify-sale-prices.md) for previewing, creating a scheduled task, and restoring original values.

## Apply the pricing workflow

For a promotion that changes the actual product price:

| Field | Before sale | During sale |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Price | $100 | $80 |
| Compare-at price | Empty | $100 |

The customer pays the reduced Price. Compare-at can display the previous price on themes that support it. Review the [Compare-at reference](shopify-compare-at-price.md) for calculations and display caveats, then follow [How to Run a Shopify Sale](how-to-run-shopify-sale.md).

Bulk Price Editor by Springify can target products or collections, preview affected variants, schedule changes, and restore saved prices after the promotion.
