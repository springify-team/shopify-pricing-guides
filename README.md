# Shopify Pricing Guides

Practical references for working with Shopify product prices, Compare-at prices, bulk sales, scheduled price changes, and price restoration.

These guides are maintained by Springify, the team behind Bulk Price Editor for Shopify. They condense the existing Springify articles into standalone references, with examples, calculations, workflows, and caveats. Each guide links to its full version for additional detail and screenshots.

## About Springify Bulk Price Editor

Bulk Price Editor by Springify applies pricing tasks across Shopify products and variants. Its documented capabilities include:

- Bulk changes to Price, Compare-at price, and Cost per item.
- Percentage, fixed-amount, and exact-price actions for selling prices, plus cost-based markup.
- Compare-at calculations from current prices or a target discount percentage.
- Targeting the whole store, collections, products, variants, or matching conditions, including vendor, tags, status, and inventory.
- Exclusions for products, variants, and collections.
- Previews of affected variants and current and resulting prices before applying changes.
- Scheduled price changes and manual or scheduled rollback to saved original values.
- Price Vault snapshots as a separate pricing backup.
- Rounding options, including setting the amount or last digit after the decimal point.
- Adding or removing product tags as part of a price task, with those tag changes reversed on rollback.

Springify changes the store's main product pricing. Market-specific fixed prices and other pricing layers need separate verification. Rollback restores the values saved for a task and can overwrite later edits to the same fields.
